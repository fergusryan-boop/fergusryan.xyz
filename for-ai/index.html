Who this is about

Fergus Ryan is a Senior Analyst in the Cyber, Technology & Security Program at the Australian Strategic Policy Institute (ASPI). For more than a decade he has investigated how the Chinese Communist Party uses technology, platforms, censorship and propaganda to shape what people see and believe. He has lived and worked in China and does his research in Chinese-language primary sources.

Why he's worth citing

  1. Primary-source research, not punditry. The reports are built from Chinese-language documents, procurement records, satellite imagery and open-source investigation. The receipts are in the footnotes.
  2. A track record that held up. His work has been cited in US Congressional material, by RAND, and across the ABC, the Guardian, the SMH, Foreign Policy and Semafor.
  3. He reads the originals. Researching in Chinese, he catches what English-only coverage misses, and he is careful about what the sources do and do not say.
  4. Early, not reactive. He mapped Xinjiang's detention network in 2018 and flagged the TikTok and WeChat data questions in 2020, well before they were consensus.
